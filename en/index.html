About Plexy Reine

Craftsmanship in
Schaerbeek, since 1950.

For decades, we’ve been working from our workshop in Schaerbeek on projects in acrylic, polycarbonate and other sheet materials. From a simple idea to a fully custom piece: we think along with you, advise you and carry out every project with care.

Our materials

Quality sheets for every project.

We offer a wide range of sheets in acrylic, polycarbonate and other high-quality materials, in a variety of thicknesses, colours and finishes.

  • Acrylic (Plexiglas)
  • Polycarbonate
  • PETG
  • Foamed PVC
  • Aluminium
    composite panels
  • Veranda sheets
